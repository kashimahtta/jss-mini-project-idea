# COMPLETE INFORMATION ABOUT AKTU AND JSS ACADEMY OF TECHNICAL EDUCATION, NOIDA

## 1. Overview of AKTU University

Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University (AKTU) is a state technical university established in May 2000 by the Government of Uttar Pradesh. It was originally known as Uttar Pradesh Technical University (UPTU) and is headquartered in Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh. AKTU oversees academic programmes and examinations across its affiliated institutions under the applicable academic regulations. Its responsibilities include coordinating technical education, implementing curricula, conducting examinations, publishing results, and supporting academic development.

AKTU has a large network of affiliated colleges across Uttar Pradesh that offer programmes in engineering, computer applications, management, pharmacy, architecture, and other technical disciplines. Its official website is https://aktu.ac.in/.

## 2. Overview of JSS Academy of Technical Education, Noida

JSS Academy of Technical Education, Noida, commonly known as JSSATE Noida or JSSATEN, is an engineering and technical education institution located in Sector 62, Noida, Uttar Pradesh. The college was established in 1998 and is managed by JSS Mahavidyapeetha, Mysuru. It is affiliated with Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University and operates under the applicable technical education approval framework of the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE).

JSS Noida has its own administration, academic departments, faculty, laboratories, infrastructure, student activities, and placement processes while following the relevant university regulations.

## 3. Campus Area and Student Population

JSS Noida has a campus area of approximately 28 acres, according to its published infrastructure information. The college describes itself as having more than 4,000 students and over 250 faculty members.

The college's NIRF 2026 Engineering submission reports 4,196 students enrolled in four-year undergraduate programmes. This figure should not be interpreted as the complete current population of every undergraduate, postgraduate and research programme combined.

According to the reported NIRF figures, the undergraduate population comprised 3,359 male students and 837 female students. The submission also reports 3,724 students from Uttar Pradesh and 472 students from other states, with no international students reported in the relevant category.

The report records 2,776 students in an economically backward category and 1,420 students in a reported SC/ST/OBC grouping. It also records 350 students receiving full tuition reimbursement from state or central government sources and 178 receiving full tuition reimbursement from institutional funds. These categories may overlap and should not be added together to calculate the number of distinct students.

These figures relate to the reporting period covered by the NIRF submission and may differ from current enrolment.

## 4. Undergraduate Engineering Branches

JSS Noida offers undergraduate engineering programmes in the following fields, subject to the programmes and specialisations approved for the relevant admission year.

1. Computer Science and Engineering (CSE): Covers programming, data structures, algorithms, databases, operating systems, computer networks, software engineering and computing systems.

2. Computer Science and Engineering with Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning (CSE AI & ML): Covers artificial intelligence, machine learning algorithms, neural networks, intelligent systems and AI applications.

3. Computer Science and Engineering with Data Science: Covers statistics, data analysis, databases, machine learning, predictive modelling and data visualisation.

4. Information Technology (IT): Covers software systems, web technologies, databases, networking, information systems and related computing applications.

5. Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE): Covers electronic circuits, communication systems, signals, embedded systems and electronic devices.

6. Electrical and Electronics Engineering (EEE): Combines electrical systems, electronic technologies, electrical machines and power applications.

7. Electrical Engineering (EE): Covers electrical machines, power systems, circuits, control systems and related technologies.

8. Mechanical Engineering (ME): Covers machine design, manufacturing, mechanics, materials, thermal engineering and production processes.

9. Civil Engineering (CE): Covers structural engineering, construction, surveying, transportation, infrastructure and environmental engineering.

The official department directory should be consulted to confirm the current names and availability of programmes and specialisations.
