About the institution

A technical education landmark in Delhi NCR

JSS Academy of Technical Education, Noida (JSSATEN) is a leading technical institution located in Sector 62, Noida — one of the region's established engineering education hubs. Since its founding, JSSATEN has built a reputation for disciplined academics, strong industry connect and a campus culture that balances rigour with growth.

  • Established in 1998
  • Located at C-20/1, Sector-62, Noida
  • Leading technical institution in Delhi/NCR
  • Affiliated to Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University

What drives us

Our Vision & Mission

To become an Institution of excellence in imparting quality Outcome Based Education that empowers the young generation with Knowledge, Skills, Research, Aptitude and Ethical values to solve Contemporary Challenging Problems.
